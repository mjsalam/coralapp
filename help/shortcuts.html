Typing at the start of a line

TypeYou get
# … ###### A heading
- or 1. A bulleted or numbered list
- [ ] A task
[ ] A checklist item (stays in its note)
> A quote
>> A line that carries down to the smaller pages inside this period, shown with ↳
``` then ↩A code block
---A divider
/The insert menu
[[Link to a note (or create one)
#nameA tag

Typing on a task line

TypeSets
@ + a dayThe planned day: @tomorrow, @fri 9am, @nov 28
< + a dayThe due date: <fri
+nameThe project
&nameThe area
#nameA tag
p1 … p4Priority, chosen from a list

Quick Capture understands more without the symbols: “call Sam tomorrow 3pm”, “due fri”, “every monday”, “!!” for priority.

Days Coral understands

You typeIt means
today, tomorrow (or tom)Those days
fri, fridayThe next Friday, never today
next friThe Friday after that
weekendThe coming Saturday
+3, in 3 days, in 2 weeks, +1mThat far from today
nov 28, 28 nov, nov 28 2027That date. Without a year, a date up to two months back is this year.
12/4, 2026-11-28That date, in your region's day/month order
add 3pm or at 10:30A time on that day

iPhone

  • Keyboard toolbar: buttons to make a task, add a date, tag, project, area or priority, add to a cove, and format the line. Select text for a formatting row.
  • Swipe right on a task line in a note to open its details.
  • Swipe left on a line to select several lines and act on them together.
  • Press and hold a line's handle to drag it.
  • In task lists: swipe from the left to complete, from the right to delete.
