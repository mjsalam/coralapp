Privacy

Sync, privacy and encryption

Coral has no account, no ads and no tracking, and there is no Coral server. Here is exactly what is protected, and how.

01

Your library lives on your devices

Your notes, tasks and everything else are stored on your Mac and your iPhone. Without Coral Pro, each device keeps its own library.

02

Syncing with Coral Pro

With Coral Pro, Coral syncs your Mac and iPhone through your own private iCloud. There is nothing to set up: sign in to iCloud on both devices with the same Apple Account, and Coral starts syncing. Settings › Sync & Privacy shows how sync is going, and Sync Now syncs straight away.

03

What is end-to-end encrypted

When you sync with Coral Pro, these are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud, with keys in your iCloud Keychain:

  • your notes — their titles and text, and every task in them with its dates and labels
  • your coves
  • your note properties
  • the amounts you log for a habit
  • an area's weekly intention

Pictures and files are sealed by Coral with AES-256-GCM before they upload. Their keys travel inside that end-to-end encryption, so neither Apple nor Coral can read the synced copies. You never type a password for any of this.

04

What uses iCloud's standard encryption

These are encrypted by iCloud, but not end to end:

  • names of projects, areas, folders, habits, goals and Currents, and their descriptions
  • whether a habit was kept on a day
  • a note's details, such as its folder and dates
  • the heading you choose for the day's Log
05

On your devices

On each device, your library is protected by the device's own encryption: FileVault on a Mac when it's turned on, and Data Protection on an iPhone with a passcode. On iPhone, the library is also part of your iCloud Backup. Apple protects that with iCloud Backup's own encryption, which is end to end only if you turn on Advanced Data Protection.

06

Backups you make yourself

Settings › Backups & Import makes a backup of your whole library, with every picture and file. Each backup is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase you choose when you make it. Keep that passphrase safe: without it, the backup can't be opened, by you or anyone else.

07

Weather

Weather on the day page is off until you turn it on in Settings › General. The privacy policy says what it, and every other outside service, receives.
