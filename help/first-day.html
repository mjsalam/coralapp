Getting started

Your first day in Coral

Coral is built around one page: today. Start there, write what's on your mind, and turn what you need to do into tasks.

01

Open Today

Today is where Coral opens. If you're somewhere else, choose Today in the sidebar (⌘1 on Mac) or the Today tab on iPhone.

The top of the page shows the date, your habits for the day, the week, and the day's tasks and events. Below that is the day's note, which is yours to write in.

02

Write

Click or tap into the note and type. It's an ordinary note: headings, lists, tables, pictures and links all work. Start a line with # for a heading or - for a bullet. Type / for a menu of everything you can add.

03

Make a task

Any line can become a task:

  • Mac: put the cursor on the line and press ⌘↩.
  • iPhone: tap the task button in the keyboard toolbar.
  • Anywhere: type /task, or start a bullet with - [ ] .

A task in a note also appears in Tasks, in Today's list and in any cove that collects it. Tick it in any of those places and it's ticked everywhere.

A checklist item is different. Typing [ ] at the start of a plain line makes a checklist item: a box that stays in its note and never shows up in your task lists. Use it for packing lists and the like.

04

Give it a day

On a task line, type @ and a day, such as @tomorrow, @fri or @nov 28. Add a time with @tomorrow 3pm. That's the day you plan to do it. For a deadline, type < instead: <fri.

Tasks you write on today's page are planned for today automatically.

05

Come back tomorrow

Tomorrow has its own page. Anything you didn't finish comes forward, so nothing is lost. Plan the day, at the top of Today, walks you through what's left and what's next.
