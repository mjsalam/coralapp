Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026 · Last updated September 12, 2026

The short version: Coral collects no personal data. Your notes, tasks, and habits live on your device and sync through your own iCloud account. We never see your content, we don't run analytics, and we don't sell anything.

01

Who we are

Coral is an independent app developed by Mark Salam. You can reach us at support@getcoralapp.com.

This policy covers the Coral app for macOS and iOS.

02

What data Coral collects

Nothing. Coral does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers — because we don't have servers.

All data you create in Coral (notes, tasks, projects, habits, calendar entries) is stored:

  • Locally in a SQLite database on your device, inside the app's private container.
  • In your iCloud account, if you have iCloud sync enabled — under your control, governed by Apple's privacy practices.

We have no access to your iCloud data. Apple's infrastructure handles the sync.

03

Encryption

Your note and task content is protected end to end when it syncs: Coral stores it in your private iCloud database using CloudKit's encrypted fields, which Apple encrypts with keys held only on your own devices — not by Apple's servers and not by us.

On your devices, Coral's database lives inside the app's private container and is protected by the system's device encryption (your passcode or password, Secure Enclave). Metadata such as due dates, labels and project assignments is stored alongside your content in the same private iCloud database.

04

Analytics and tracking

Coral contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no behavioral tracking of any kind.

We do not use:

  • Third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • Advertising networks or identifiers
  • Crash reporters that transmit personally identifiable information
  • Session recording or heatmap tools

If crash reporting is added in a future version, it will be opt-in and will transmit only anonymized diagnostic information — no note content, no identifiers.

05

System permissions

Coral requests access to certain system features only when you use them:

  • Calendar — to display your events alongside your planner. Read and write access, only if you grant it.
  • Reminders — to import tasks from Reminders. Optional.
  • Location — only if you choose to tag a note or entry with a location. Never accessed in the background.
  • Health (iOS) — to log habit completions as health data. Optional; requires explicit permission.

None of this data leaves your device except through your own iCloud account.

06

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your private CloudKit container (iCloud.com.atoam.Coral). This is your personal iCloud storage — Coral has no access to it. Apple's privacy policy governs how iCloud data is handled.

iCloud sync is optional. Coral works fully offline without it.

07

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. There are no third parties that receive your data as part of Coral's operation.

The only exception would be if required by law — in which case we could only produce what we have: nothing, because we don't store your data.

08

Children's privacy

Coral is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through Coral, please contact us so we can address it.

09

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. We will never retroactively weaken your privacy protections.
