What's inside

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Coral brings together the tools you use every day — without the tab-switching, the import/export, or the subscription creep.

Notes & Writing

A fast block editor with inline markdown, code blocks, callouts, and toggles. Organize notes into folders or link them to projects.

Tasks & Projects

Tasks live inside notes — no separate system to maintain. Group them under projects, assign deadlines, track status, and see progress at a glance.

Areas & Life Balance

Organize everything into life areas — Work, Health, Finance, Personal — and see a radar chart of where you're spending your energy.

Calendar & Planner

A daily and weekly planner that connects to your system calendar. Plan your day alongside your notes and tasks — everything in context.

Habits & Streaks

Build routines with daily habits, streak tracking, and completion views. Habits live in the same world as your notes and tasks.

The Reef

An organic knowledge graph that surfaces connections between your notes — backlinks, clusters, orphaned ideas — visualized as a living map.

Privacy

Local-first. Encrypted. Yours.

Your data never touches our servers. It lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud — end-to-end encrypted, always.

End-to-end encrypted

Sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it ever leaves your device.

No tracking, ever

No analytics, no ad networks, no behavior tracking. We do not collect or sell your data.

Your iCloud, your sync

Sync happens through your private iCloud account. Apple's infrastructure, under your control.
